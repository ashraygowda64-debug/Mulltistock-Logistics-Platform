API ENDPOINTS:
  • GET  /products/api/ - List products with filters
  • POST /products/api/create/ - Create product
  • POST /products/api/update/{id}/ - Update product
  • GET  /products/api/detail/{id}/ - Product details
  • GET  /products/api/categories/ - List categories
  • GET  /products/api/suppliers/ - List suppliers
  • POST /stock/api/adjust/ - Adjust stock
  • POST /stock/api/transfer/ - Transfer stock
  • GET  /stock/api/movements/ - Movement history
  • GET  /api/customer/ - Customer CRUD
  • GET  /api/supplier/ - Supplier CRUD
  • GET  /api/warehouse/ - Warehouse CRUD

SECURITY:
